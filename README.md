### About me

I'm a full-stack developer from Brazil focused on **AI software engineering** and **AI automation**. I design and ship products where LLM agents do real work: provisioning servers, running trading strategies, triaging production errors, and automating business operations.

- 🏢 Founder at **[INTUS HUB](https://intushub.com.br)**, where we build AI, crypto and business automation
- 🤖 Working daily with **OpenClaw**, **Hermes** and **Claude Code** agents
- 🧱 I care about typed boundaries, observability and boring, reliable infrastructure
- 🌎 Based in Brazil, building for Brazil/LATAM and beyond
